Guide

Plain-English glossary, method and limits

MQL, SQL, opportunity

A lead sales has reached and judged worth pursuing is a marketing-qualified lead (MQL). When sales opens a deal it becomes an opportunity. A won opportunity is a customer.

Speed-to-lead

Hours between a lead arriving and sales first contacting them. Interest fades quickly, so slow follow-up loses deals.

Net revenue

First-year contract value from customers who did not cancel or get refunded within 90 days.

Benchmark

The conversion rate at the 75th percentile of your own channels at each stage. It shows what is already achievable in-house.

AI lead score

Gradient-boosting model using channel, segment, device, form length, speed-to-lead, touches and first touch. Leads are ranked by expected net revenue.

Last-touch vs first-touch

Last-touch credits the final channel before conversion; first-touch credits the channel that started the journey; linear splits credit equally.

Method. (1) Count each stage by channel, device and month. (2) Value leaks as channel net revenue × (benchmark ÷ actual − 1) at each stage from Lead onward. (3) Score leads with a model trained on months 1-9 and tested on months 10-12. (4) Estimate fixes with model counterfactuals (speed, form length) and a diminishing-returns budget model.

Limits. Estimates come from observed data, not experiments; validate the big moves with A/B tests. The budget model assumes revenue rises with spend^0.5 and caps receiving channels at +60%. Levers overlap slightly, so combined gains are reduced by 10%. This site shows synthetic demo data; swap in your own and rebuild.
